Cancel your gym membership – template & guidance

A regular (ordinary) cancellation of a gym membership ends the contract at the next possible date, provided you observe the notice period agreed in your contract. You are not required to give a reason – it's recommended to keep the tone polite and free of accusations.

New contract-law rules since March 2022

Since 1 March 2022 stricter statutory limits apply to new contracts (in the UK/EU these typically reflect consumer-friendly terms; check your national rules). As a rule of thumb for new agreements:

  • Maximum initial term: 12 months
  • Maximum auto-renewal if you don't cancel: 3 months (often 12 months in older contracts)
  • Maximum notice period: 1 month before the contract end date

For contracts signed before March 2022, the old terms continue to apply – check your contract for the exact notice period.

What the cancellation letter must contain

  • Full name and address
  • Membership number (if you have one)
  • Desired end date, or "at the next possible date"
  • Request for written confirmation
  • Revocation of the direct-debit mandate

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to give a reason for cancelling?

No. With an ordinary cancellation, you are not obliged to state a reason. You should, however, avoid criticism in your letter – a factual tone is more professional and prevents unnecessary conflict.

What if I miss the notice deadline?

Still send your cancellation – using the wording "at the next possible date". For post-March-2022 contracts the term only extends by up to 3 months, not a full year as before.

How should I cancel – by letter or email?

Many gyms accept cancellation by email or via an online form. The legally safest option, however, is a written letter sent by registered post with proof of delivery, because you can prove it was received on time.

Can I cancel early (extraordinary cancellation)?

Yes, for important reasons (relocation, illness, price increase, change of opening hours). See the template for extraordinary cancellation for details.
